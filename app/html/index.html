Sign-in
Sign in here when the portal needs a one-time code or single sign-on. The camera keeps the gateway session and reconnects on its own until the gateway ends it.
The portal uses single sign-on. Complete it in your browser:
  1. (opens in a new tab)
  2. Sign in there, including any MFA prompt.
  3. When the portal says the sign-in is complete, click the Send to camera bookmark in that tab. Create it once by dragging the button to your bookmarks bar (show the bar with Ctrl+Shift+B, or Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac).
Without the bookmark: on the final page, copy the globalprotectcallback: link, or view the page source and copy all of it, then paste it here.
